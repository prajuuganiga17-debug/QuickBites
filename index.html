Our Story

About QuickBite

QuickBite was born in 2020 with one powerful vision: to make great street food accessible to everyone, everywhere, at any time.

What started as a small startup with just 10 restaurant partners in Bangalore has grown into one of India's fastest-growing food delivery platforms — operating across 50+ cities, partnering with 500+ restaurants, and supported by a fleet of 10,000+ dedicated delivery partners.

We have delivered over 5 million meals — and we are just getting started. Our platform is built on three non-negotiables: trust, transparency, and a deep, unshakeable passion for food.

Our Core Values

  1. Quality First — We partner only with restaurants that meet our strict 50-point food quality and hygiene standards.
  2. Speed & Reliability — Every delivery step is optimised so your food arrives on time, every single time.
  3. Customer Happiness — Your satisfaction is our only measure of success. Always.
  4. Sustainability — We use eco-friendly packaging and support green delivery initiatives across all cities.
  5. Community — We invest in local restaurants and empower small food entrepreneurs to grow their business.
